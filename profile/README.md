<h3 align="center">zPredict</h3>
<p align="center">Shielded prediction markets on Zcash. Price public, participants private.</p>

Bet on an outcome without anyone learning that you bet, which side you took, or how much.

FROST 2-of-3 escrow · orders secret-shared across 3 relayers · YES/NO matched in MPC · batched Ironwood payouts.
